## Bug fixes

- **Environment dropdown no longer overflows the viewport.** On self-hosted [BI Platform](/bi-platform/self-host) deployments, the environment dropdown could extend past the right edge of the screen and clip its contents. It now repositions itself to stay fully within the viewport.
