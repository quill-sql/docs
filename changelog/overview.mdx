<Update label="June 8, 2026">

## Bug fixes

- **Loading indicator while updating charts** — The [Chart](/react/chart), [SQLEditor](/react/sql-editor), and [ReportBuilder](/react/report-builder) components now show a spinner while a chart is being saved or updated. This makes it clearer when changes are still in flight and prevents users from making edits before the previous update completes.
