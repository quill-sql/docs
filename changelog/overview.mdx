<Update label="June 2, 2026">

## Updates

**New onboarding flow for the Quill portal**

Signing in to [app.quill.co](https://app.quill.co) no longer creates an organization for you automatically. Instead, you'll see a clearer landing screen when your account isn't tied to a Quill environment yet, with two paths forward:

- **Accept a pending invite.** Any pending organization invites on your account are listed so you can switch into an existing team in one click.
- **Create a new organization.** A "Create new" button takes you to [the onboard flow](https://app.quill.co/onboard) so you can set up an environment intentionally.

This prevents accidental duplicate organizations when teammates sign up separately and makes it easier to join the right org the first time. See the [BI Platform quickstart](/bi-platform/quickstart) for the full onboarding walkthrough.

## Fixes

**Database-scoped tenants persist correctly**

When connecting a schema with database-level tenant scope, the tenant field is now saved reliably even if the backend response omits it. This fixes cases where a tenant could appear misconfigured immediately after onboarding. See [Tenant](/bi-platform/tenant) for tenant scoping details.

</Update>
