## June 3, 2026

### New features

**Preview AI agent edits before applying them**

The AI chat agent now shows a diff summary of every change it wants to make to your [dashboards](/bi-platform/dashboard), [reports](/bi-platform/report), and [virtual tables](/bi-platform/virtual-table) before anything is saved. You can review additions, updates, and deletions grouped by dashboard, then accept or discard the proposed changes. This makes it safer to iterate with the agent on production content.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue where the selected environment was not preserved after a hard browser refresh. Your active environment now sticks across reloads.
