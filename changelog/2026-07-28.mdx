## Bug fixes

- **Smoother chart animations.** Charts in the React SDK no longer trigger unnecessary re-renders when animating, resulting in smoother transitions and better performance on dashboards with many charts.
- **`isAnimationActive` now works on all chart types.** The animation toggle is now correctly wired through bar, line, pie, and radar charts, so you can disable or enable animations consistently across every [`Chart`](/react/chart) and [`StaticChart`](/react/static-chart).
